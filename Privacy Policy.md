# **SharkEye Privacy Policy — Revised and Annotated Draft**

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026 

## **Introduction**

This Privacy Notice explains how the Benioff Ocean Science Laboratory ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with SharkEye, a desktop application that uses machine learning to detect and analyze great white sharks and other objects in aerial footage.

**Using SharkEye does not, by itself, require users to provide personal information to our team.** Users may choose to submit shark footage, images, detection data, or feedback. Information is shared with the team only when users choose to submit it through an available sharing or feedback feature, subject to the technical practices described below.

## **Summary of Key Points**

* **Ordinary use:** No personal information is required to be shared with the SharkEye team simply to use the desktop app.  
* **Optional research submissions:** Users may voluntarily share footage, images, detection outputs, and related research data (such as shark measurements and annotations).  
* **Optional feedback:** Users may submit feedback through the in-app feedback form.  
* **Purpose:** Voluntarily submitted information may be reviewed to support research, assess detections, improve the app, and respond to feedback, as applicable.  
* **No automatic transfer:** The team does not receive users' locally processed footage or detection results solely because the app is used unless a user chooses to enable automatic uploads.

## **1\. What Information Do We Collect?**

### **Information processed locally**

SharkEye processes videos to provide its analysis features. Processing files within the desktop app does not, by itself, mean those files are submitted to the SharkEye team.

### **Information users choose to share**

Within the app, users may voluntarily submit:

* Video footage and stills, including aerial drone footage of sharks and surrounding environments;  
* Detection outputs, annotations, measurements (including shark lengths), the location at which videos were taken, and other related research information;  
* Feedback messages

## **2\. How Do We Use Submitted Information?**

We use information that users voluntarily submit for purposes related to the submission, which may include:

* Reviewing shark footage, images, detections, and related scientific data;  
* Training new detection models;   
* Evaluating detection quality and improving SharkEye's functionality;  
* Reviewing feedback.

## **3\. When and With Whom Do We Share Information?**

Information submitted to SharkEye is received by the team and may be handled by the systems used to deliver the submission. 

## **4\. Do We Offer Artificial Intelligence-Based Features?**

Yes. SharkEye uses machine-learning models for image and video analysis, including object detection. The presence of AI functionality does **not** mean that users' footage or detection results are automatically uploaded to the SharkEye team, unless manually enabled by the user.

## **5\. How Long Do We Keep Submitted Information?**

We retain voluntarily submitted footage, detection data, and feedback only according to our actual research, operational, and legal retention practices.

## **6\. How Do We Protect Submitted Information?**

We aim to apply appropriate safeguards to information users choose to submit. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.
